Cheese-Stuffed Meatballs

Ground beef pockets oozing melted mozzarella, pan-seared in butter and finished in marinara.

20 mins
25 mins
480 cal
38g
4g
34g

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1
    In a large mixing bowl, combine ground beef, grated parmesan, egg, minced garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Mix gently until combined.
  2. 2
    Divide the beef mixture into 12 equal portions. Flatten each portion into a disc, place a mozzarella cube in the center, and wrap the meat completely around the cheese.
  3. 3
    Heat butter in a heavy cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add meatballs in a single layer.
  4. 4
    Sear for 6-8 minutes, turning frequently until all sides are golden brown.
  5. 5
    Pour sugar-free marinara sauce into the skillet, lower heat to medium-low, cover with a lid, and simmer for 10 minutes until cheese is gooey.

💡 Chef's Ranch Tip

Use 80/20 ground beef for maximum juiciness, and chill stuffed meatballs for 10 minutes before searing to prevent cheese leaks.

Bacon-Wrapped Chicken Thighs

Smoky bacon crisps around juicy thigh meat, roasted until the fat renders golden.

15 mins
35 mins
520 cal
42g
2g
38g

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a wire rack over a baking sheet.
  2. 2
    Pat chicken thighs dry. Season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3
    Wrap two slices of bacon tightly around each chicken thigh, tucking ends underneath.
  4. 4
    Bake for 30-35 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F (74°C). Broil 2-3 minutes until bacon is crispy.

💡 Chef's Ranch Tip

Broil for the final 3 minutes to get ultra-crispy bacon edges without overcooking the chicken.

Loaded Cauliflower Bake

Riced cauliflower baked with sharp cheddar, sour cream, and crumbled bacon.

15 mins
25 mins
310 cal
14g
6g
25g

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1
    Steam cauliflower florets for 6-8 minutes until tender-crisp. Drain well.
  2. 2
    In a bowl, beat cream cheese, sour cream, minced garlic, half the cheddar, and half the bacon until smooth.
  3. 3
    Fold in cauliflower, transfer to an 8x8 baking dish, and top with remaining cheddar and bacon.
  4. 4
    Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

💡 Chef's Ranch Tip

Drain steamed cauliflower thoroughly in a towel to ensure a creamy casserole instead of a watery dish.
